Witches Of Starfall

Role: Developer

Team: 2

Language:Unity

Duration: 4 years (Intermittent)

Completion: Ongoing

Work In Progress

This project is a passion project for myself and my artist friend. This game is currently getting a rework with the code starting from scratch. This is due to my recent knowledge gain in C# developement and the original code being over 2 years old now. This game will be a cosy RPG where the player joins a magical town and attends Grimms' School of Magic where they will complete learning tasks such as growing plants for potion crafting, defeat slimes for magical items and learn spells to name a few.

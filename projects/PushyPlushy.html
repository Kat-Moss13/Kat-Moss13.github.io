Pushy Plushy

Role: Gameplay Programmer

Team: 24 Developers

Language: C++/Blueprints

Engine: UE5

Duration: 8 Weeks

Completion: Finished

Awards: Finalist Tiga Best Student Game 2025

Open Steam Page

Overview

Pushy Plushy is a cute, chaotic and fast-paced 2-4 player party game where no two games play the same. It was made as part of a university module designed to reinact a small studio from art to programming. Our goal was to create a short experience that could be replayable with the additions of modifiers to change how every minigame ran.

My Contributions

I made the multiplayer system with controller support for my team and was the overseer for all C++ integration into the project. I prototyped two minigames, paint dash and coin collectors before moving over to the C++ team. Additionally I worked on the points UI and the UI art integration so I worked closely with the lead 2D artist. Around week 5 all dev work had been done and it was bug resolving for the rest of the time.
